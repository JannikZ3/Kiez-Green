Berliner Stadtgüter

Managing Berlin's green heritage — sustainably, for everyone.

Who is Berliner Stadtgüter?

Berliner Stadtgüter GmbH is the public enterprise responsible for managing Berlin's municipal agricultural and green spaces. With a history stretching back decades, BSG stewards hundreds of hectares of productive land surrounding the city — from market gardens and orchards to wetlands and forest edges.

Through Kiez Green, BSG opens these spaces to local communities — connecting residents with foresters, coordinating volunteering, and creating shared ownership over Berlin's green future.

Our Representatives

BSG is represented in each community area by a dedicated forester and community liaison. These professionals are your first point of contact for questions about land management, planned works, and ecological initiatives in your area.
